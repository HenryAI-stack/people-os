**1.0.1**: Picks up the next Teams call by itself after a call ends, and adds a **Rescan** button
(⟳ in the one-line view) to look for a new call window manually. Captions of a new call are never
merged into the previous call's lines.

Caption Grabber keeps the live captions of a Microsoft Teams call and copies them as a transcript.

- Works with the new Teams desktop app and with Teams in Edge or Chrome.
- One self-contained exe for Windows 10/11 (64-bit). No installer, no admin rights, no network access.
- Verify the download against `CaptionGrabber.exe.sha256`.

See `desktop/caption-grabber/README.md` in the repository for what the app does and doesn't do.
