Teams Caption Grabber

Collects Teams live captions while you talk, then copies the whole conversation in one click so you can paste it into the PeopleOS interview. It is a bookmark, so nothing gets installed.

Add it to your browser

[once] Drag the yellow button onto your bookmarks bar.

▤ Caption Grabber

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B. If dragging doesn't work: copy the code, create a new bookmark, name it "Caption Grabber" and paste the code into the URL field.

In every call

  1. Join in the browser, not the desktop app

    Open the meeting at teams.microsoft.com in Edge or Chrome. The bookmark can only read captions in a browser tab.

  2. Turn on live captions

    More (…) → Language and speech → Show live captions. Set the spoken language there if it isn't detected correctly.

  3. Click the Caption Grabber bookmark

    A small panel appears bottom right and counts lines as people speak. You can hide it with ×; it keeps capturing.

  4. After the call: Copy transcript

    Paste into the PeopleOS interview notes. "Download .txt" saves a file as backup.

[11:00] Max: Thanks for joining. Could you walk me through your last role?

[11:03] Max: What was the hardest part of that change?

Good to know

Keep the tab open until you've copied. The transcript is also autosaved in the browser; if the page reloads, click the bookmark again and choose "Restore earlier".

Quality equals Teams' captions. Names and technical terms come out as Teams heard them, so give the text a quick read before saving.

Check your company's rules and tell the candidate. If transcription is switched off by policy, capturing captions may need the same approval and consent a recording would.

If nothing is captured, Teams may have changed its page structure. Tell Claude and the selectors can be updated.
